About BrakeMaster

Pioneering mobile brake repair since 2015. Our mission is to deliver professional, convenient, and affordable brake services right to your doorstep.

Our Journey

What started as a single mobile van in downtown Chicago has grown into a nationwide network of certified brake specialists. We saw a gap in the market — drivers needed quality brake repair without the hassle of towing or dealership wait times.

Today, BrakeMaster completes over 15,000 brake repairs annually, maintaining a 4.9-star rating from satisfied customers. Our mobile-first approach saves you time and money, with transparent pricing and premium parts.

Our Process

We've streamlined mobile brake repair to be effortless for you. Book online or call, choose your location, and our certified technician arrives fully equipped. We perform a complete brake inspection, share digital estimates, and only start work after your approval.

All parts come with a 12-month/12,000-mile warranty. We test-drive your vehicle and provide a detailed service report before leaving. Simple, transparent, professional.
